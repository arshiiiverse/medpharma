About this demo

Medpharma on this website is a fictional concept store built as a portfolio project by Arshdeep Kaur. No real orders are placed, no payments are taken and no medical advice is given. The text below shows what these policies would cover for a real online pharmacy.

Privacy policy

We collect only the information needed to process your orders and bookings:

  • Contact details — name, mobile number, email and delivery address.
  • Health information — prescriptions and test bookings, used only to fulfil your request.
  • Usage data — pages visited and items viewed, to improve the store.

Health information is never sold or shared for advertising. It is shared only with the pharmacist, doctor or lab handling your request. In this demo, everything you enter stays in your own browser (localStorage) and can be cleared at any time.

Terms of use

  • Prescription medicines are dispensed only against a valid prescription from a registered medical practitioner.
  • Prices and availability can change without notice. The price at checkout applies.
  • Online consultations do not replace emergency care. In an emergency, call 112 or visit the nearest hospital.
  • Product information is for general guidance. Always read the label and follow your doctor's advice.

Returns & refunds

  • Unopened, non-medicine items can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
  • For safety, opened or temperature-sensitive medicines cannot be returned.
  • Damaged, expired or wrong items are replaced or refunded in full — report them within 48 hours.
  • Refunds are processed within 3–5 working days to the original payment method.

Questions? Contact our care team.
