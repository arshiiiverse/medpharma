Our story

Medpharma started with a simple frustration: getting a prescription filled, a blood test done and a doctor's opinion meant three different trips across the city.

We built one place for all of it. Today, families across the Tricity order medicines, book home sample collection and talk to doctors on video — and every order is checked by a licensed pharmacist before it leaves our store.
